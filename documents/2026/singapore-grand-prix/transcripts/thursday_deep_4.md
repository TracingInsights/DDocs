Team, though, say they've found the issue and they've fixed a few components and everything should be OK with the fuel system. So this weekend, hopeful, back to normal or not?

**LN:**

Yeah, hopeful. Need to get on track first and see how much better it is. It's obviously also a circuit where straights are not the highest priority and not as detrimental as the last few weekends. But nevertheless, you always want to go down the straights as quick as what you should be able to. And it's always tough when you come out of corners better and end up going slower. So, it's been a frustrating two weekends trying to figure out what the issue's been and why there's just been a constant lack of straight-line speed and performance. And especially in this kind of battle we've been in, the last two qualifyings it's been two or three positions. So, it's cost me quite a lot. At the same time, there's obviously things I can do better as well. But confident that the team have done a good job. They put a lot of work into trying to figure out things and hopeful that it'll be better this weekend. But honestly, I have to get out on track and see first.

**Q:** (Panagiotis Seitanidis – Ant1 TV) Another one for Lando, if I may. The spotlight is always on you drivers, but what you can do is most of the time limited to what the car can do. Although you're a world champion already, I feel that this season you're driving the best, you do the best drives you ever did. How does it feel to have the audience not knowing that you did a great drive because you finished fourth, but you feel that you have done an excellent job?

**LN:**

There's not a lot you can do about that, honestly. It's the same with everything in life. You're kind of just judged off of the visual performance, especially from TV, more than what your, I think especially in Formula 1, honestly, more than just what your own performance has been, because the car might not be as quick on one weekend compared to another. So, I guess you just hope that people understand that. I guess I hope that you guys and girls understand that. You know, everyone in F1 is in F1 for a reason, and just because the car is not as good doesn't mean that another driver is worse or better, and vice versa. So, it's always difficult from that point of view. And yes, there's a lot of races this year I feel like I've driven better and been able to extract more. And I'm not saying that last year I did a bad job. It's just I'm talking about being able to extract 99% versus 99.5%, and it's just those last little things. The three qualifyings I've been on pole have been by, I think, under half a tenth every time. Like, two hundredths, three hundredths, we're talking just very, very small margins. But those three pole positions gave me chances to win three races. If I was not on pole position, unlikely I would have won any other races. So, that's just how quickly a little margin can change the whole outlook on something. But yeah, I mean, the end of last year, the second half of last year, I think some of my performance was just as good as this year. I think this year I have to drive the car in a different way, and I've had to adapt a lot to how you have to drive this year's car. And I think there's just two skill levels to it. There's the general skill level of just extracting performance out of the car itself, and there's also extracting performance out of the power unit and the driving and the tyres and all of this, this kind of extra little level. And I think that's what I've done a very good job on this year and been able to understand quite quickly. But I'm not fussed if people on the outside see it as good or not. I think it's more important that, for me and my team, I'm satisfied. And my job every weekend is to extract the maximum out of myself. If I can do that, I'm happy. I'd be happier if it was for a win, but you have to realise your goals in Formula 1 are not always winning the race outright. A win for us this weekend could be a P5 or a P3. So, I think you have to acknowledge what the maximum performance for your own team is in your own car, and that's all you can really be happy with at the end of the day.
