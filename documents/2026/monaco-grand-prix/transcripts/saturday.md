FIA complete seven-day candidate event, assessing Rally US preparations for potential inclusion in the FIA World Rally Championship calendar. The assessment covered sporting, safety and medical operations, including observation of an American Rally Association round and visits to proposed stages, service park and ceremonial locations in Kentucky and Tennessee. With the candidate event now complete, attention turns to the FIA’s post-event reporting process, the outcome of which will determine Rally US’s eligibility for consideration for the FIA World Rally Championship calendar by WRC Promoter.
