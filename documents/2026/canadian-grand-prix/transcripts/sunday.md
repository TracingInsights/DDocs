This November, the Circuito de Madrid Jarama is set to host the debut test of the GEN4 cars, ushering in Formula E's latest era. Following the inaugural 2026 CUPRA Raval Madrid E-Prix held in March, the ABB FIA Formula E World Championship will return to the iconic track in Spain’s capital to host pre-season testing for the first time. Pre-season testing will see the GEN4 car hit the track in a collective test session for the first time ahead of the 2026/27 Season of the ABB FIA Formula E World Championship.
