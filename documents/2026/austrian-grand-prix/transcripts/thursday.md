The Extraordinary General Assemblies (EGA) took place in Macau for the second year running, hosted by the Automobile General Association Macao-China (AAMC), and supported by FIA Official Sponsor Galaxy Entertainment Group The event was once again held at the iconic Galaxy International Convention Centre where the EGA will also be hosted in 2027 The 2025 Annual Report & Accounts were approved and published in full, delivering the strongest set of financial results in a decade Proposed amendments to the FIA Statutes and Internal Regulations were approved by the EGA
