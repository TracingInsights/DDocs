## TECHNICAL DELEGATE'S REPORT

### The following driver will use a new a new turbocharger (TC) for the remainder of the Event:

### Number Car Driver Previously used TC

### 27 Renault Nico Hülkenberg 4

The turbocharger used by Nico Hülkenberg is the fifth new turbocharger for the 2018 Championship season and this is not in conformity with Article 23.3a of the 2018 Formula One Sporting Regulations.

The Renault Sport Formula One Team informed the technical delegate about the turbocharger change on 24 August 2018, at 17:32 hours.

As this is the second new turbocharger introduced by Nico Hülkenberg at this Event, which is subject to a grid penalty, only this turbocharger may be used at subsequent Events without incurring a penalty according to Aricle 23.3 b) of the 2018 Formula One Sporting Regulations.
