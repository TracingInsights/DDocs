## Technical Delegate's Report

The following driver will start the nineteenth Event of the 2018 Formula One World Championship with a new internal combustion engine (ICE):

## Number Car Driver Previously used ICE

10 Toro Rosso Honda Pierre Gasly 7

The internal combustion engine used by Pierre Gasly is the eighth new internal combustion engine for the 2018 Championship season and this is not in conformity with Article 23.3a of the 2018 Formula One Sporting Regulations.
