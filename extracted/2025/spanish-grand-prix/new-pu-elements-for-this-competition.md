## 2025 SPANISH GRAND PRIX

## 30 May - 01 June 2025

From : The FIA Formula One Technical Delegate To : The Stewards Date : 30 May 2025

### Technical Delegate's Report

The following driver will start the ninth Competition of the 2025 Formula One World Championship with a new internal combustion engine (ICE):

## Number Car Driver Previously used ICE

43 Alpine Renault Franco Colapinto 3

The internal combustion engine used by Franco Colapinto is one (1) of the four (4) new internal combustion engines allowed for the 2025 Championship season and this is in conformity with Article 28.2 of the 2025 Formula One Sporting Regulations.

The following driver will start the ninth Competition of the 2025 Formula One World Championship with a new exhaust system (EX):

## Number Car Driver Previously used EX

43 Alpine Renault Franco Colapinto 4

The exhaust system used by Franco Colapinto is one (1) of the eight (8) new exhaust systems allowed for the 2025 Championship season and this is in conformity with Article 28.2 of the 2025 Formula One Sporting Regulations.
