## Technical Delegate's Report

The following drivers will start the eighth Event of the 2019 Formula One World Championship with a new internal combustion engine (ICE):

## Number Car Driver Previously used ICE

33 RBR Honda Max Verstappen 2 10 RBR Honda Pierre Gasly 2 03 Renault Daniel Ricciardo 2 18 Racing Point Mercedes Lance Stroll 2 26 Toro Rosso Honda Daniil Kvyat 3
