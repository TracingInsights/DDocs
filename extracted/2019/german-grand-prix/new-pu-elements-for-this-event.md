## Technical Delegate's Report

The following drivers will start the eleventh Event of the 2019 Formula One World Championship with a new motor generator unit-kinetic (MGU-K):

## Number Car Driver Previously used MGU-K

16 Ferrari Charles Leclerc 1 08 Haas Ferrari Romain Grosjean 1 20 Haas Ferrari Kevin Magnussen 1 07 Alfa Romeo Ferrari Kimi Räikkönen 1 99 Alfa Romeo Ferrari Antonio Giovinazzi 1
