The Stewards, having received reports from the Race Director, have considered the following matters and determine the following:

Session Practice 2

Fact The cars below did not use the track at the exit of turn 10.

| Decision | Car | Driver | Competitor | Offence time | Lap Time(s) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 33 | Max Verstappen | Aston Martin Red Bull Racing | 15:53:39 Next Lap | 1:03.991 Pit Lane |
| 2 | 20 | Kevin Magnussen | Hass F1 Team | 16:04:00 Next Lap | 1:10.292 1:10.528 |

| 4 | 10 | Pierre Gasly | Scuderia AlphaTauri Honda | 16:34:34 Next Lap | 1:09.986 1:10.025 |

Offence Breach of Appendix L, Ch IV, Article 2c of the FIA International Sporting Code and failure to follow the Race Director’s Race Notes 21.2.a).

Decision Deletion of the lap times shown in accordance with Article 12.3.1.e of the FIA International Sporting Code and Art 31.4 of the FIA Formula One Sporting Regulations.

Reason The Stewards reviewed video and received timing information from the Race Director.

The cars left the track. The penalty was applied in accordance with point 21.2. a) of the Race Director’s Event Notes and Article 12.3.1.e of the FIA International Sporting Code.

Competitors are reminded that they have the right to appeal certain decisions of the Stewards, in accordance with Article 15 of the FIA International Sporting Code and Article 10.1.1 of the FIA Judicial and Disciplinary Rules, within the applicable time limits.
