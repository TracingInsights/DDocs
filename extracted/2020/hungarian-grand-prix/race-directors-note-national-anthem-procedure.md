NOTE TO TEAMS: NATIONAL ANTHEM PROCEDURE

Please note that the procedure for the National Anthem in Hungary will be significantly different to normal – taking place in the Pit Lane, not at the front of the Grid.

Drivers will be required to assemble in front of the giant LED screen underneath the Podium. The National Anthem will begin at 14:54, please ensure that your drivers are aware of the new location and are in position at least 30 seconds before the ceremony begins.

Drivers may use the gates at either Grid Position 6 or Grid Position 18 to go from the grid to the National Anthem area.

Please see the attached National Anthem Diagram.
