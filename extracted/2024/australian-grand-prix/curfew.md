From : The FIA Formula One Technical Delegate To : The Stewards Date : 23 March 2024

### Technical Delegate's Report

Last night team personnel of the Williams Racing team, who are associated with the operation of the car, were within the confines of the circuit during the twelve and a half (12.5) hour period which commenced at 21:00 on 22 March, fifteen and a half (15.5) hours before the scheduled start time of P3 and ends three (3) hours before the scheduled start time of P3 at 09:30 on 23 March.

This was the first of the two (2) individual exceptions permitted for the Williams Racing team during the 2024 Formula One Championship season and therefore no action should be taken.
