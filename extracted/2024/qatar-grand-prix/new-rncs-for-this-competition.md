### Technical Delegate's Report

The following driver will start the twentythird Competition of the 2024 Formula One World Championship with a new gearbox case and cassette (GBX C & C):

**Number Car Driver Previously used GBX C & C**

44 Mercedes Lewis Hamilton 3

The gearbox case and cassette used by Lewis Hamilton is one (1) of the five (5) new gearbox cases and cassettes allowed for the 2024 Championship season and this is in conformity with Article 29.2 of the 2024 Formula One Sporting Regulations.

## Number Car Driver Previously used GBX DL

44 Mercedes Lewis Hamilton 3

The gearbox driveline, gear change components and auxiliary components used by Lewis Hamilton is one (1) of the five (5) new gearbox drivelines, gear change components and auxiliary components allowed for the 2024 Championship season and this is in conformity with Article 29.2 of the 2024 Formula One Sporting Regulations.
