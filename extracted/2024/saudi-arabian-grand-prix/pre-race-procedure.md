## 2024 SAUDI ARABIAN GRAND PRIX

### 07– 09 March 2024

### From The FIA Formula One Media Delegate

### To All Officials, All Teams Date 09 March 2024

Please find below a summary of the requirements for the Pre-Race activities and National Anthem, which must be followed in order to ensure the orderly running of these procedures.

19:44:00 Drivers move to National Anthem Position. For the duration of the National Anthem all Drivers must remain attired only in their race suits.

### 19:46:00 The National Anthem.

Failure to comply with these procedures will be reported to the Stewards.

Please see the attached National Anthem Diagram.

**Roman De Lauw The FIA Formula One Media Delegate**
