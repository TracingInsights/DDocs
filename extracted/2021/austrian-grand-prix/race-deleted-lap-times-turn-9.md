Offence Breach of Appendix L, Ch IV, Article 2c and 12.2.1 (i) of the FIA International Sporting Code, failure to follow the Race Director’s Event Notes, point 21.

Decision Deletion of the lap time shown in accordance with Article 12.4.1.e and 12.2.1 (i) of the FIA International Sporting Code.
