## TECHNICAL DELEGATE'S REPORT

### The following drivers will start the third Event of the 2021 Formula One World Championship with a new gearbox:

### Number Car Driver

### 77 Mercedes Valtteri Bottas 63 Williams Mercedes George Russell

The above drivers did not finish the last race in Imola and are in conformity with Article 23.5a of the 2021 Formula One Sporting Regulations.

### Number Car Driver

### 22 AlphaTauri Honda Yuki Tsunoda

The above driver had to replace his previous gearbox during the last Event for a spare gearbox and this was before the six consecutive Events expired. Therefore he may use a new gearbox at this Event and the six consecutive Events will start here in accordance with Article 23.5a of the 2021 Formula One Sporting Regulations.
