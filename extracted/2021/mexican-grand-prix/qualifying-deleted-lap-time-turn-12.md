From The Stewards Document 42

To All Officials, All Teams Date 6 November 2021

Time 15:35

The Stewards, having received a report from the Race Director, have considered the following matter and determine the following:

Session Qualifying

Fact The car below did not use the track at turn 12.

| Decision | Car | Driver | Competitor | Offence time | Lap Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 47 | Mick Schumacher | Uralkali Haas F1 Team | 14:03:13 | 1:21.524 |

Offence Breach of Appendix L, Ch IV, Article 2c of the FIA International Sporting Code and Art. 27.3 FIA Formula One Sporting Regulations.

Decision Deletion of the lap time shown in accordance with Article 12.4.1.e of the FIA International Sporting Code and Article 31.4 of the FIA Formula One Sporting Regulations.

Reason The Stewards received timing information and a report from the Race Director.

The car left the track and the driver set his fastest lap time. The penalty was applied in accordance with Article 31.4 of the FIA Formula One Sporting Regulations.

Competitors are reminded that they have the right to appeal certain decisions of the Stewards, in accordance with Article 15 of the FIA International Sporting Code and Chapter 4 of the FIA Judicial and Disciplinary Rules, within the applicable time limits.
