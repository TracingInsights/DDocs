The Stewards, having received reports from the Race Director, have considered the following matters and determine the following:

Session Race

Fact The cars below did not use the track at turn 2.

| Decision | Car | Driver | Competitor | Offence time | Lap Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 47 | Mick Schumacher | Uralkali Haas F1 Team | 15:09:28 | 1:44.586 |
| 2 | 63 | George Russell | Williams Racing | 15:35:09 | 1:41.915 |
| 3 | 77 | Valtteri Bottas | Mercedes-AMG Petronas F1 Team | 16:10:39 | 1:41.316 |

Offence Breach of Appendix L, Ch IV, Article 2c and 12.2.1 i of the FIA International Sporting Code, failure to follow the Race Director’s Event Notes 22.

Decision Deletion of the lap time shown in accordance with Article 12.2.1 i of the FIA International Sporting Code.

Reason The Stewards received timing information and reports from the Race Director.

The cars left the track. The penalty was applied in accordance with point 22 of the Race Director’s Event Notes (reference Article 12.2.1.i of the FIA International Sporting Code).

Competitors are reminded that they have the right to appeal certain decisions of the Stewards, in accordance with Article 15 of the FIA International Sporting Code and Chapter 4 of the FIA Judicial and Disciplinary Rules, within the applicable time limits.
