# 2026 MONACO GRAND PRIX

- **05 – 07 June 2026**

**From The Stewards Document 105 To All Teams, All Officials Date 22 June 2026**

- **Time 11:45**

The Stewards, having received a Petition for a Right of Review, from Mercedes-AMG PETRONAS Formula One Team, dated June 12, 2026, and having now been advised by the Competitor that is wishes to withdraw its Petition.

**DECIDE that the Deposit of €20,000, required for the Petition, be returned to the Competitor.**

**Reasons** In accordance with Article 14.4.3 of the FIA International Sporting Code, the Stewards consider that the circumstances dictate the return of the Deposit.

The above Decision is subject to appeal in accordance with Article 15 of the FIA International Sporting Code and Chapter 5 of the FIA Judicial and Disciplinary Rules, within the applicable time limits.

Decisions of the Stewards are taken independently of the FIA and are based solely on the relevant regulations, guidelines and evidence presented.

**Garry Connelly Tanja Geilhausen Derek Warwick Jean-François Calmes The Stewards**
