## Number Car Driver Previously used PU-ANC

18 Aston Martin Aramco Honda Lance Stroll 8

The power unit ancillary component used by Lance Stroll is the ninth (9th) of the six (6) new power unit ancillary components allowed for the 2026 Championship season and this is not in compliance with B8.2.2 g) of the 2026 Formula One Sporting Regulations. All PU elements for car number 18, driver Lance Stroll, have been replaced without the approval of the FIA technical delegate, this not being in compliance with Article B3.5.4 of the 2026 Formula One Sporting Regulations.

Changes to the suspension set-up of car number 18 have been made without the approval of the FIA technical delegate. Therefore car number 18 should now be required to start the Race from the pit lane according to Article B3.5.3 a) of the 2026 Formula One Sporting Regulations.

Car numbers 18 was not covered 2 hours after the end of Qualifying, respectively 5 hours before the scheduled start of the formation lap for the Race, this not being in compliance with Article B3.4.3.

I am referring these matters to the stewards for their consideration.
